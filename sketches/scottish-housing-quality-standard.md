# Scottish Housing Quality Standard (SHQS)

Found by Faye. The benchmark social landlords (including Cloch) must meet.

Source: [gov.scot technical guidance](https://www.gov.scot/publications/shqs-technical-guidance-for-social-landlords/)

A home fails the standard if it fails any **one** of these five criteria:

1. **Meets the tolerable standard** — the legal baseline for being fit to live in (structurally stable, free from damp, proper lighting/heating/ventilation).
2. **Free from disrepair** — key parts of the building (roof, walls, windows, etc.) aren't in a state of disrepair.
3. **Energy efficient** — reasonable thermal insulation and efficient heating.
4. **Has modern facilities and services** — kitchen and bathroom reasonably modern and well laid out.
5. **Healthy, safe and secure** — fire safety, carbon monoxide/smoke alarms, secure entry.

Underneath these sit 55 detailed technical elements that assessors check off. The Scottish House Condition Survey tests 54 of them (the only one skipped is external noise insulation). Source: [gov.scot methodology notes](https://gov.scot/publications/scottish-house-condition-survey-methodology-notes-2019/pages/5)

**For the challenge:** Cloch reports 94.0% of homes meeting SHQS (Scottish average 89.5%) — [Scottish Housing Regulator](https://www.housingregulator.gov.scot/landlord-performance/landlords/cloch-housing-association-ltd/). SHQS is about the building meeting a standard, not about who lives there or their health risk — worth remembering, since it won't tell us which tenants are at risk from damp/mould even in a home that technically meets the standard.
