# Whiteboard notes: first idea for Healthier homes

Photos of the squad's paper notes are in this folder. Typed up here so they're
searchable and easy to build from.

## People demographics — people

- Literacy + language
- Financial ability
- Age
- Families
- Disabilities / health conditions
- Vulnerability
- Tech literacy

## Criteria — building

- Type of building
- Age
- Location — rural / town / coastal
- Previous issues
- EPC band — energy band
  - How efficient you are to keep energy in
  - Diminishes over time
- Solidity of walls
- Ventilation
- Exposure to weather
- Failed / missing damp proof course

## Assumptions

- Similar house types are likely to experience the problem at the same time.
- Separate landlords — siloed, not one organisation → one landlord, CLOCH.
- Do they know each other, to pass on information?
- If damp is caused by a neighbour's house, Cloch are not responsible.
- Already have a MyCloch app where tenants can report issues.
- Shared data from trade people going into the houses — plus other groups who
  interact with people.
- Nothing proactively flags who is at risk before they report it.
- Employee side of the service — what do they need?
- For our solution — how much data can be shared, and with who?

## Ideas

- Property info gives a risk indication.
  - No data on certain properties — we can use Claude to make assumptions.
- Understand culture + societal norms + reasons they don't report.
- Where are their touchpoints?
  - Local support places
  - Who are the groups they trust and work with?
  - Who do they trust?
  - How do we reach them?
- Education + QR codes + ...

## Problems

- People don't tell / report it.
- Monitoring is too expensive in every house.
- People can't heat.
- People can't ventilate.
- Over 1,000 homes — 1,446 homes — spread out over separate areas.
- Do people understand what the problem is?
  - Illnesses
  - Gradual increase
  - Signs to spot
- Early signs: peeling paint, dehumidifiers in the house.
- They don't have choice.
