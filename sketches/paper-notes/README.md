## Problems ([problems.jpg](problems.jpg))

- People don't tell / report it
- Monitoring is too expensive in every house
- People can't heat
- People can't ventilate
- More than 1,000 homes (1,446 homes), spread out over separate areas
- Do people understand what the problem is?

  - gradual increase
  - signs to spot
- Early signs: peeling paint, dehumidifiers in the house
- They don't have choice

## Criteria: building ([criteria-building.jpg](criteria-building.jpg))

- Type of building
- Age
- Location: rural / town / coastal
- Previous issues
- EPC band (energy band)
  - how efficient you are at keeping energy in
  - diminishes over time
- Solidity of walls
- Ventilation
- Exposure to weather
- Failed or missing damp-proof course
