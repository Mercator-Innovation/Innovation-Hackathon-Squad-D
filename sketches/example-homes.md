# Example homes (made up)

Twelve **made-up** homes for the Healthier homes prototype. They're not real Cloch homes, and the addresses and areas are invented. Gathered by Joe Passi.

The features come from the research on what affects damp risk: age, solid walls, cold surfaces and ventilation, exposure to weather, and the damp-proof course. The mix roughly follows Cloch's real stock (mostly 2 and 3 apartment general needs homes) from the Scottish Housing Regulator.

There's no risk score here on purpose. How to judge risk is for the squad to decide, with a person making the final call.

## Things to notice

- **H01 and H05** have many risk features together: old, solid walls, poor ventilation, exposed, and damp before.
- **H11** is the challenge sheet's warning in action: a modern, energy-efficient home that has still had damp. The building doesn't explain why, which suggests the tenant may not be able to afford to run the heating or ventilation. That's a guess for the squad to explore, not a fact.
- **H06** shows what improvement looks like: the same street as H05, but with wall insulation and fans.

## Notes

- "Apt" is the Scottish count of rooms, not counting the kitchen and bathroom.
- This is about homes only. It deliberately holds no information about the people who live in them.
- When the prototype is ready, this can become a data file in `src/data/`.
