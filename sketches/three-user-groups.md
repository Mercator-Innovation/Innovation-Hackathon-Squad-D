# Three user groups, not one "housing staff"

Found by Faye, talking it through. Worth the squad discussing before building more.

The challenge sheet groups everyone as "Cloch's staff: housing officers, repairs teams, and the energy advice and community support teams" — but for the tool, they split into three roles with three different needs:

1. **Field visitors** — out in homes already (repairs, energy advice, community support on a routine visit). They spot a problem and need to **add it to the tool**, on the spot, probably on a phone.
2. **Housing officers** — look at the tool to **decide who to help first** (this is who the current prototype is built for: the map, the list, the score, the "your decision" panel).
3. **Support and repairs staff** — go back into the home to **fix the problem or support the household**, based on what an officer decided. They need to know what they've been asked to do, and to mark it done.

**What's missing today:** the prototype only serves role 2. There's no way for role 1 to add a new report, and no way for role 3 to see their assigned actions or close them out. The "decide" options (visit, phone, refer, book repair) are an officer's call *about* someone else's work — but that work doesn't currently go anywhere.

**Related:** earlier research found UK housing officers and repairs/energy-advice staff mostly work out in the field, not at a desk (Hillcrest Housing Association gave officers tablets for exactly this reason). Roles 1 and 3 here are the ones most likely to be on a phone or tablet, not a laptop — worth keeping in mind if either gets built.
