# Hacky McHackface: 2-minute outline

Drafted by Faye. About 250 words, roughly a minute of silent reading at talking pace.

## What we built, and for whom (0 to 60s)

- **Healthier homes.** Cloch Housing Association can't tell which tenants are most at risk from cold, damp or mould until someone reports a problem. We built a tool so Cloch's staff can proactively target their most vulnerable tenants, before health issues or repairs arrive.
- **Show it:** open the prototype (the risk map and the "homes to look at first" list), or play the journey animation first — https://claude.ai/artifact/DND9Gop97p8effw1tDUiY9 — it shows how we got from a blank whiteboard to this in one afternoon.

## One thing that didn't work (60 to 90s)

- Midway through, the live site suddenly showed "undefined" everywhere after a round of merges, and it looked like something had broken. It turned out to be nothing more than a stale browser cache from testing. A hard refresh fixed it instantly. Lesson for next time: when a lot of people are sharing work at once, refresh before you panic.

## One surprise from the tooling (90 to 120s)

- Joe, our content designer, ended up doing almost none of his "usual" job. He researched damp risk factors and offline tenants, built a working HTML sketch of a new screen, redesigned the review page, split the whole site into accessible tabs, and designed the squad's logo, wobble animation included. None of that is content design. It's everything the squad needed, done by whoever picked it up.
